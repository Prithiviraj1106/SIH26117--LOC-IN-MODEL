MEMORANDUM

To: Engineering Department
From: Maintenance Supervisor
Date: January 15, 2024
Subject: Centrifugal Pump Inspection Findings - Q4 2023

During the fourth quarter inspection cycle, the following observations were noted for centrifugal pumps across all units:

1. Pump C-101: Bearing temperature consistently running 5°C above baseline. Recommend bearing replacement during next planned outage.
2. Pump C-204: Minor seal leak detected at mechanical seal. Seized pump for repair on January 10, 2024.
3. Pump C-302: Vibration levels at 3.1 mm/s, exceeding the 2.5 mm/s threshold. Alignment check required.

Recommended actions:
- Schedule bearing replacement for C-101 in Q1 2024 shutdown
- Complete C-204 repair and verify seal integrity
- Perform alignment correction on C-302 before Q1 operations

All findings documented in the maintenance management system (CMMS).
