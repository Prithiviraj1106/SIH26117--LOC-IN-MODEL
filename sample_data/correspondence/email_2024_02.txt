MEMORANDUM

To: Operations Manager
From: Safety Officer
Date: February 3, 2024
Subject: Safety Audit Results - Pump House Area

2. LOTO procedures correctly followed in 98% of observed maintenance activities
3. Two instances of missing PPE compliance — corrective action issued
4. Fire suppression system inspected and certified
5. Ventilation system operating within specifications

Recommendations:
- Continue monthly PPE compliance checks
- Schedule fire suppression system test for March 2024
- Review ventilation system filters quarterly

Next audit scheduled for August 2024.
