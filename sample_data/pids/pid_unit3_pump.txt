P&ID — Unit 3 Pump Assembly
Document Number: P&ID-U3-PA-001
Revision: B
Date: February 2024

COMPONENTS:
- Pump C-101 (Centrifugal, 50 HP)
- Motor M-101 (50 HP, TEFC)
- VFD VFD-101 (50 HP)
- Suction Strainer SS-101
- Discharge Valve DV-101 (Globe, 6" ANSI)
- Isolation Valve IV-101 (Gate, 8" ANSI)
- Pressure Transmitter PT-101
- Temperature Transmitter TT-101
- Vibration Sensor VT-101
- Relief Valve RV-101 (set at 150 PSI)

PIPING:
- Suction line: 8" NPS, from suction tank T-101 to pump suction
- Discharge line: 6" NPS, from pump discharge to header PH-03
- Vent line: 2" NPS, to atmospheric vent
- Drain line: 2" NPS, to drain tank DT-101

CONTROL LOGIC:
- Auto-start on demand signal from PLC
- Speed control via VFD (0-60 Hz range)
- High temperature alarm at 85°C, shutdown at 90°C
- High vibration alarm at 4.0 mm/s, shutdown at 5.0 mm/s
- High pressure alarm at 120 PSI
- Low suction pressure alarm at 20 PSI
