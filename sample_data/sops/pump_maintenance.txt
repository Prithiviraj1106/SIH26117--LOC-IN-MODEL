Standard Operating Procedure: Centrifugal Pump Maintenance

Section 1: Safety Precautions
- Always lock out and tag out (LOTO) before performing maintenance
- Verify zero pressure in the suction and discharge lines
- Wear appropriate PPE including safety glasses and gloves

Section 2: Inspection Checklist
- Check bearing housing temperature — should not exceed 80°C
- Inspect mechanical seal for leaks
- Verify coupling alignment within 0.002 inches
- Check vibration levels — maximum allowable 2.5 mm/s
- Inspect base plate bolts for tightness

Section 3: Recommended Maintenance Intervals
- Daily: Visual inspection of leaks and unusual noise
- Weekly: Bearing temperature check and vibration monitoring
- Monthly: Alignment check and lubrication
- Annually: Full overhaul including impeller inspection

Section 4: Emergency Procedures
- If bearing temperature exceeds 85°C, shut down immediately
- If seal leak is detected, isolate the pump and tighten or replace seal
- In case of abnormal vibration, check for foreign objects in the suction
