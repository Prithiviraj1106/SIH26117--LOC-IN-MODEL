Standard Operating Procedure: Control Valve Operations

Section 1: Valve Classification
- Gate valves: Used for on/off isolation, not for throttling
- Globe valves: Used for flow regulation and throttling
- Butterfly valves: Used for large diameter isolation and modulation
- Ball valves: Quick shut-off, suitable for high pressure systems

Section 2: Operational Guidelines
- Operate valves slowly to prevent water hammer
- Never exceed the maximum allowable pressure (MAWP) rating
- Verify valve stem packing is intact before operation
- Check for proper valve positioning after actuation

Section 3: Maintenance Requirements
- Monthly: Stem lubrication and visual inspection
- Quarterly: Seat and seal inspection
- Annually: Full valve overhaul with replacement of gaskets and seals
- After any emergency shutdown: Full inspection before restart

Section 4: Calibration
- Control valves should be calibrated every 6 months
- Use certified pressure gauges for calibration reference
- Document all calibration results in the maintenance log
